Definitions and keys

walkable means ALL of these hold: (a) the cell is connected to the main walkable region, not an isolated patch in a random spot; (b) a dwarf can be displayed there; (c) it looks walkable in the image; (d) a walker could stand there; (e) it can be selected as a tile the dwarf can move to. If any one fails, leave walkable unchecked and, if you like, tick the reasons. Row 9 cell centres sit under the bottom 8 px HUD.

fungus: fungal growth visible at the centre (cap, stalk, gills, shelf fungus, mycelium). harvest_candidate: you would want this to be a small collectable resource. This is your design preference, not a fact about the pixels. water: exposed water at the centre (not water hidden under a bridge deck). built_structure: something constructed at the centre (boards, bridge deck, steps, masonry, furniture).

Keys: arrows move; click the scene to jump; 1-5 toggle labels; Q W E R T toggle walkable reasons; Enter marks the cell done, saves and moves on; K skip; N notes (Esc leaves notes); G grid; O authored overlay. A cell is only exported once you mark it done or change something; untouched cells stay blank, never "no". Cells outlined in orange are labeled walkable but not connected to the largest walkable region.
